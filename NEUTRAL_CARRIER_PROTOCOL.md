# Neutral-carrier rival test

Version 2026-07-14-v1. The competing explanation is that the early reusable
controller is a context-neutral lexical/value-state delta, rather than a
binding-relevant state. Build raw L2 value prototypes only from the neutral
non-binding carrier `Here is a token: VALUE.`. Compare its raw target-minus-
source direction to the already established raw `Let` controller when each is
injected at the queried value slot of the mapping grammar on held-out offsets
5/7.

No fit, rescaling, alignment, layer selection, or carrier variation is allowed.
The controls are the neutral wrong-value direction at the queried slot and the
neutral correct direction at the other binding slot. A neutral match requires
behavioral accuracy >= .80, positive neutral effect in >= .80 rows,
neutral/natural and neutral/Let ratios in [.70, 1.30], and a .10 margin above
both controls. A match supports the lexical-state account; a non-match, while
the `Let` baseline remains positive, supports binding-context information in
the early controller under this measurement.
